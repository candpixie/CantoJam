協音cantojam

Cantonese has six tones, and a melody that moves against them turns your words into different words. Type lyrics below to see the shape their tones already demand, then drag any note and find out the moment the melody starts fighting the language.

Drag a note to move it, or focus one and use ↑ ↓. Hover a red or amber connector to see what the corpus says. Your draft saves itself in this browser, and Copy link puts the whole thing in a URL. There is no server and no account: nothing you write is ever uploaded.

The melody already exists

If you wrote the tune first, paste it here instead of dragging notes into place. One melody line per lyric line, in numbered notation (1 2 3 5, where ' lifts an octave, . drops one, # and b bend a semitone and 0 is a rest) or in note names (F4 G4 A4). A MIDI export works too, and is split across the lines in order. Nothing is uploaded: the file is read in this browser.

What the corpus actually says
Where each tone sits, in semitones relative to a song's median pitch. Two things fall out that the traditional 0243 ladder gets wrong: tone 5 sits with tone 3 rather than with tone 6, and tone 4 is a cliff, roughly three semitones below everything else.
